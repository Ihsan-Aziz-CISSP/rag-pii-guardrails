MT Corp AI Acceptable Use Policy v1.2

Section 1: Approved Tools
Employees may use ChatGPT Enterprise for drafting emails and summarizing documents.

Section 2: Prohibited Data
Never input the following into any LLM:
- Customer PII: Names, emails, phone numbers. Example: John Smith, john.smith@acme.com, 555-123-4567
- Employee HR data: SSN, salary. Example: Jane Doe, SSN 123-45-6789, $125,000 salary
- PHI: Patient medical records per HIPAA. Example: Patient ID P-789, Diagnosis: Hypertension
- API Keys: Example sk-proj-abc123xyz789

Section 3: Incident Response
Report suspected data leakage to security@acme.com within 1 hour.

Section 4: Training
Annual training required. Last training completed by HR Manager Sarah Johnson on 03/15/2026.
